# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

The primary audience is technical recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Nicholas Perez's
modern C++ engineering. They should be able to understand the application's purpose and
architecture quickly, operate it without prior instruction, and inspect a codebase whose
quality extends beyond the visual demonstration.

## Product Purpose

OrbitLab is an interactive three-dimensional N-body gravity workbench. It makes orbital
mechanics tangible while demonstrating portable native development, numerical simulation,
performance awareness, reproducible numerical experimentation, testing, persistence, and
clean C++20 architecture. Success means
the application behaves correctly, communicates its model clearly, and remains easy to
build and verify.

## Positioning

A compact scientific instrument whose visible behavior is backed by inspectable,
well-tested engineering.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, and inquisitive. The interface should feel like a trustworthy laboratory
instrument: calm enough to study, dense enough to be useful, and free of theatrical
"space game" decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon arcade styling, gratuitous glow, unreadably dense telemetry, glass panels,
oversized decorative controls, and visuals that obscure numerical behavior. Do not make
the interface depend on color alone.

## Design Principles

- Make the system legible before making it impressive.
- Keep simulation state and user actions explicit.
- Let real motion and data provide the visual interest.
- Use familiar controls and direct feedback.
- Make engineering claims verifiable through tests, documentation, and deterministic behavior.
- Present experimental methods with an explicit hypothesis, validated domain, and failure record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, supplement color with labels and selection outlines, provide
keyboard shortcuts for essential transport controls, and avoid decorative motion beyond
the simulation itself.
